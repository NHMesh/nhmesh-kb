# Code of Conduct

NHMesh is a hobbyist radio community. Most of us are learning as we go, and the
knowledge base exists because people were willing to admit what they didn't know.
Keep that possible.

## Expected

- Assume good faith. A wrong guide is usually someone's outdated best effort, not
  carelessness.
- Critique the content, not the contributor. "This setting changed in 2.5" is
  useful; "did you even read the docs" is not.
- Beginners' questions are the point. Half the guides here exist because someone
  asked something basic in Discord.
- Respect that everyone here is a volunteer, including maintainers and reviewers.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, personal attacks, or demeaning comments about anyone.
- Discrimination on the basis of who someone is.
- Publishing others' personal information, including node locations tied to a home
  address, without their consent.
- Deliberately introducing content that would harm readers, their equipment, or
  the network.
- Sustained disruption of discussion or review.

## Scope

This applies in this repository — issues, pull requests, reviews, and commits.
The NHMesh Discord and Matrix spaces have their own moderation; conduct there is
handled there, though serious problems may affect participation in both.

## Enforcement

Report concerns to the maintainers privately: open a
[security advisory](https://github.com/NHMesh/nhmesh-kb/security/advisories/new)
if you'd rather it not be public, or contact a maintainer directly on Discord.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close pull requests, and block accounts.
We'll aim to explain a decision to the person affected, and we'll take the safety
of the person reporting seriously — including their preference to stay anonymous.

Amateur radio operators: note that FCC Part 97 rules on obscenity, encryption,
and business use apply to what you transmit regardless of anything written here.
